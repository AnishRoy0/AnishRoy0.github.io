VIII. SHTEM Research

Motivation

Gastric cancer is the fourth leading cause of cancer-related deaths worldwide. If the gastric cancer is Epstein-Barr Virus (EBV) Associated gastric cancer, patients have better prognosis and those with EBV-associated gastric cancer respond better to immunotherapy. It is therefore pivotal to be able to identify EBV in pathology whole slide images (WSIs) of gastric cancer patients.

Goal

We used a foundation machine learning model to determine whether a WSI of a gastric cancer patient has EBV or not. Our cohort worked with Dr. Lee in the Ji research group for this task.

Results

Our model performed at an AUROC of 0.8625, though we quickly realized that the model was overwhelmingly predicting EBV negative since most samples in the dataset were EBV negative. To fix this, and by doing so, improving the recall & F1-Socre results, we implemented downsampling, and our AUROC improved to 0.9093.
